Dendrite browser activity

Reports only the active tab in the focused window to your local API. It never reads page contents, screenshots, or keystrokes. Private/incognito tabs are excluded. URLs omit credentials, queries, and fragments; page paths and titles may still be sensitive.

Dendrite's Stop recording control takes precedence. Keep this extension disabled in browser profiles you do not want to record. Backend exclusions also apply; adding exclusions does not erase old data.
